DATA for R code 'WP2 power analysis'
- 'Astley 2016 - Table S2' - the results for calculating results in 'Astley null relationships'.
- 'LOTSS tree' - a literature-based tree of phylogenetic relationships used for plotting, using Portik et al 2023 as a
  baseline relationships for newly discovered miniaturised species added.
- 'Study taxa.csv' - list of species being targeted, with the general field site(s) they are found in and how difficult
  they are to find.

RESULTS
- 'Astley null relationships', plots of jumping, walking and swimming performance against body size with estimations of
  performance in miniaturised frogs by extrapolating the regression line.
- 'All_Traits_Deviation_Power_Analysis.csv' - whether differences small (20%), moderate (50%) and substantial (80%)
  differences from the null relationships calculated from Astley 2016's data can be detected with the simulated number
  of different species and individuals for locomotor trials, based on a minimum power of 0.8.
- 'Final_sampling_design_optimisation.csv' - Calculations of phylogenetic diversity (PD) for each combination of potential
  field sites (therefore species) that cover at least the minimum viable sampling size, taking the difficulty of finding
  each species into account.
- 'Site_loss_contingency_analysis.csv' - The combination of sites with the best balance of available species, PD, and
  difficulty was Seychelles, Andasibe, Marojejy and Montagne d'Ambre. These data are the results of a simulated sensitivity
  analysis where the loss in PD is calculated for the loss of each field site with and without replacement. This represents
  a set of contingency plans in the case that a field site becomes unavailable.
